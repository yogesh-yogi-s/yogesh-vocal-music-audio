Versioned contracts

VMA Format Guide

VMA is a container, not a codec and not an AI separator.

V1

PCM representation

Canonical WAV workflow

Stores PCM samples from two valid RIFF/WAVE sources with timing and audio properties.

  • Mono or stereo PCM WAV
  • 8, 16, 24, or 32-bit PCM
  • Synchronized playback
  • Canonical WAV extraction
V2

Opaque representation

Original payload preservation

Stores accepted source bytes without decoding or transcoding.

  • Exact supplied payload bytes
  • Format and role descriptors
  • Direct per-stream extraction
  • Codec-dependent preview
Important limitationA filename cannot guarantee browser decoding. VMA does not derive stems from mixed recordings.

Practical guidance

Help / FAQ

Answers about containers, playback, security, and local sessions.

What is a VMA file?

A versioned container keeping separate audio payloads, roles, timing, and metadata together.

How do I create one?

Choose Vocal and Music / Instrumental sources. PCM WAV pairs create V1; other accepted files create V2.

How is my audio handled?

No account is required. Audio is processed in temporary, process-local storage; inactive sessions expire after two hours and cleanup runs periodically. Use Delete session to revoke the active session immediately. A server restart also removes this ephemeral state.

Why will a V2 stream not play?

The current browser may not decode its codec. The exact source remains downloadable.

Does VMA separate vocals?

No. Supply stems that already exist separately.

Will navigation stop playback?

No. Workspace navigation preserves the active client session and playback.
